# Alex Karp came to Palantir from a German doctorate in social theory

Alexander C. Karp co-founded Palantir Technologies, with Peter Thiel among others, and runs it as chief executive. His path there is unusual for a software CEO: a BA at Haverford (1989, in philosophy, by Wikipedia), a law degree at Stanford (1992), then a doctorate at Goethe University Frankfurt (2002).

Checked against the two sources above only. Wikipedia gives both 2003 and 2004 for the founding, so the year is left out here.
