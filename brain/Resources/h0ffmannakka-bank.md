# h0ffmann/akka-bank

Small demo project for Akka, initiated originally by Heiko Seeberger @hseeberger Akkounts, its use case is bank accounts: deposit, withdraw and get the balance. Akkounts is a distributed system making use of Akka Cluster. It employs Event Sourcing and CQRS enabled by Akka…
