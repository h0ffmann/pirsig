# Ouroboric: open factories that improve themselves

Ouroboric builds open factories that improve themselves: for software, companies, and in time
research and art. The ideas are Bruno Valério's. The owner's line for it: "i'm always reverse
engineering my own interests", "that what makes me ouroboric".
