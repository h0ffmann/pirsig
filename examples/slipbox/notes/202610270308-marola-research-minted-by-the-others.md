# marola-research was minted by the other repositories

One directory is one research: its paper, its sources, the log of how each source was checked,
its notes, its journal, its site. "The discipline comes from the root; each research is its
owner's." Its rules came from closed-source, ww3-gpu, picasso and marola.

The clearest case of [[202610270321-discipline-flows-from-the-root]].
