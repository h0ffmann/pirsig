# Four lights

Every work of the owner takes one of four lights: blue for water (the sea, and what is measured or
built for it), green for ideas (knowledge worked out and written down), orange for Ouroboric, red
for closed source. One thing, one light, set by hand.

In Pirsig the four lights are the four areas of the PARA map (see `docs/use-cases.md`). The graph
they colour: [[202610270316-the-works-hold-each-other]].
