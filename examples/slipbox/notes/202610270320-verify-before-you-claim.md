# Verify before you claim

The same rule appears in five repositories, each in its own form:

- closed-source: cite what you use, and a tool that checks every reference ([[202610270302-paper-fabric-checks-every-reference]]);
- marola-corpus: one source per note, and what does not hold is deleted, not softened ([[202610270306-marola-corpus-one-note-one-source]]);
- ptbr-writer: `⚠` on what it did not check ([[202610270311-ptbr-writer-marks-what-it-did-not-check]]);
- gcp-agentic-architect: every claim `(v)` or `⚠` ([[202610270315-gcp-agentic-architect-marks-every-claim]]);
- the profile's slip box: `status: unverified | partial | verified | approved` ([[202610270316-the-works-hold-each-other]]).

For ztk: a note's status is a first-class field, and a claim is born `unverified`.
