# marola, an ocean intelligence layer

marola gives sea conditions and bathing-water quality for Brazilian beaches, made with five
friends, as citizen science. The umbrella's tooling is 100 % Rust; the app is Scala.

It sits in the middle of [[202610270323-the-water-pipeline]].
