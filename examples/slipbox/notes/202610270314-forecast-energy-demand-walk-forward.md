# forecast-energy-demand: one walk-forward protocol for ten models

An undergraduate thesis at UFRJ: ten forecasting algorithms on hourly PJM electricity demand, all
under the same walk-forward cross-validation (672 h window, 168 h horizon), each run with and
without weather covariates to isolate what the weather adds, and benchmarked against GEFCom2014.
