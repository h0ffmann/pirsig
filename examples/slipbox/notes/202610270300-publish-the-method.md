# Publish the method, give it an identifier, cite what you use

closed-source is a book, *closed source: the god that failed*, and the tool that builds it. Its
thesis: closed code asked researchers to trust the secret, and between 2024 and 2026 that trust
failed in public, since agents take programs apart and questions are enough to copy models. The
honest path is to publish the method, give it an identifier and cite what you use.

Why science cannot be the exception: [[202610270301-science-never-closes]]. The tool that holds the
book to it: [[202610270302-paper-fabric-checks-every-reference]].
