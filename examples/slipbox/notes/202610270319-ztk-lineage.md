# Where ztk comes from

smart-notes ("notes on rails") is archived and points to ouroboric-dev/zettelkasten ("2nd brain
from the future"). h0ffmann/zettelkasten is a public fork of it, and ztk is a Loco app; Loco calls
itself "Rust on Rails", and h0ffmann/loco is a fork of it too. "On rails" survived the rewrite.
