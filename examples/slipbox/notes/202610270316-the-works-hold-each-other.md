# The works hold each other

The knowledge graph of the profile is not a drawing of what feels related: an arrow is a tie that
a record states (a Zenodo record names another work as cited, referenced or required, or a paper
lists it in its references). `edges.tsv` holds eleven of them, from closed-source, marola, ww3-gpu
and marola-bench.

This slip box keeps the two kinds apart: a stated tie is `verified`, a tie that a reader saw is
`partial` until the owner approves it ([[202610270320-verify-before-you-claim]]). Each work has a
colour: [[202610270317-four-lights]].
